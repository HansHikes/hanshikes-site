Values, rules and terms

How Han's Hike Club works

A quick note from me

✨ Hikes are £10 (plus a 20p booking fee), booked on CLIQ.

✨ I'm a lone soldier, so please be patient if info doesn't arrive at the click of a button.

✨ We "soft hike": lovely long walks (we don't have many mountains in the UK 🧗🏼).

🌳 The main group is for sharing anything outdoorsy, fun, recommendations, questions, photos or ideas. If it involves fresh air or good vibes, it belongs here.

📆 For each monthly hike I create a separate spin-off group, so you only get the info you need if you're coming along. No notification overload.

📍 All key hike details are always posted in the main group first. You join the hike using the link in the info message. Easy peasy 🍋

👥 Each hike is limited to 20 people. This keeps things friendly, chatty and connected, and stops us overwhelming tiny villages when we all descend on cafés and pubs at once.

🚕 I arrange carpooling where I can, to save the planet and make journeys easier.

☀️ The whole idea is connection over crowd. Meeting new people, having great conversations, and enjoying the outdoors together (with minimal screen time).

Terms and conditions

Refund

If I cancel or move a hike, for weather or anything else, you get the £10 back (minus the 20p transaction fee).

No refund

If you can no longer make it, or don't turn up on the day.

  1. Booking and paymentHikes are £10 plus a 20p booking fee (£10.20 in total), booked and paid for on CLIQ. The 20p covers the card processing fee from Stripe, which handles payments securely. Your place is confirmed once payment goes through, and each hike is capped at 20 people.
  2. If I cancel or rescheduleIf I cancel a hike for any reason, including bad weather, safety or illness, you'll get a 100% refund of the hike price, minus the 20p transaction fee, which Stripe doesn't return. If I move it to a new date, you can keep your place on the new date or take the same refund.
  3. If you can't make itYour £10.20 isn't refundable if you cancel or don't turn up, as it covers planning and bookings made for the group. You're welcome to give your place to a friend: just let me know at least 24 hours before so I can update the group and the pub booking.
  4. WeatherWe walk in most weather, so dress for it. If conditions are unsafe, such as storms, heavy snow or official weather warnings, I'll cancel and let everyone know in the hike group as early as I can, usually the day before.
  5. Fitness and kitOur hikes are soft hikes, but they're still 2 to 5 hours on uneven ground. Please check the distance and make sure it suits you. Wear proper walking shoes or boots, and bring water, snacks and layers. If you have anything I should know about to keep you safe, message me privately before the hike.
  6. Taking partYou take part at your own risk and are responsible for your own belongings. Han's Hike Club holds public liability insurance. Nothing in these terms limits liability where the law doesn't allow it.
  7. Travel and carpoolingYou're responsible for getting to the start and home again. Train times are a guide, so please book your own tickets. Carpools are arranged between members as a favour, and it's fair to offer drivers a contribution towards fuel.
  8. Food and drinkCoffee stops and pub lunches aren't included in the £10 and are paid for separately. If I ask for meal pre-orders, please send yours by the deadline.
  9. AgeHikes are for adults aged 18 and over, unless an event says otherwise, like our Bring a Parent hike.
  10. PhotosI take photos on hikes to share on Instagram and in the Community. If you'd rather not appear, just tell me on the day and I'll leave you out.
  11. Looking after each otherBe kind and inclusive, follow the Countryside Code, and leave every path, café and pub as you found it. Members are vetted, and I may remove anyone from a hike or the Community, without a refund, if they make it feel unsafe or unwelcoming.
  12. RetreatsRetreats have their own booking terms, including deposits and payment dates, which I'll share when bookings open.
  13. Changes to these termsI may update these terms from time to time. The version on this page when you book is the one that applies.

Good questions

How fit does my team need to be?

Not very. We choose a route for your group's fitness, with a shorter option built in that we can switch to on the day. As long as your team can walk for at least 3 hours, they're good to go.

What if it rains?

We walk in most weather and adjust the route if needed. If conditions are unsafe, we reschedule at no extra cost.

What should people wear and bring?

Comfortable footwear with grip and a waterproof layer. Everyone gets a joining pack with a full kit list.

How far from London are the routes?

Usually around an hour to an hour and a half by train.

Can you cater for dietary needs?

Yes. We pre-order lunch with the pub, so just share dietary needs 10 days before the hike.

Our team is fully remote. Does that work?

Perfectly. Everyone meets at the start point, and it's often the first time people meet in person.

What's included in the price?

Planning, hosting, the route, pub booking and pre-order management, the joining pack and all the little touches. Food, drink and travel are extra.

Are you insured?

Yes. Han's Hike Club Ltd is a registered company with public liability insurance, and every hike has its own written risk assessment.
